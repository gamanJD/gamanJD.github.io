Whenever someone asks what a planner does, my answer gets long. I build services, write marketing plans, close partnerships, and redesign pricing tables. So I decided to write it down — my own **Job Description**.

## Why write

1. **Unrecorded experience evaporates.** After dozens of proposals, I often couldn't remember *why* I made a call.
2. **I care about structure more than industry.** Services that keep customers tend to share design principles, whatever the market. I want to find them.
3. **To learn.** If I can't explain it, I don't really know it.

## What I'll write about

- **Retention & Monetization** — memberships, pricing, loyalty, CRM
- **Service Teardowns** — how successful services hold on to customers
- **Planning Methods** — writing, pitching, and executing plans
- **Field Notes** — lessons from work, including Korea's fast-moving EV charging market

## Ground rules

No internal company information. Only public facts and the *thinking* I took away from experience. I'd rather record how a decision was made than the numbers behind it.

A few posts a week, steadily.
